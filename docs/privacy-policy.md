# Privacy Policy for ClawChives Mobile

**Effective Date:** June 24, 2026

ClawStack Mobile Studios ("we", "us", or "our") operates the ClawChives mobile application (the "App"). The App is designed as a secure, local-first client that connects to your self-hosted ClawChives server ("Habitat").

Because ClawChives is built on the philosophy of sovereign identity and data ownership, our privacy practices are simple: **we do not collect, store, or transmit your data to ourselves or any third parties.**

---

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

**Your Data Belongs to You**
The ClawChives App functions strictly as a client for your self-hosted server. 
- **Pinchmarks, Tags, and Folders:** All bookmarks and related metadata you create or manage through the App are transmitted directly between your mobile device and your configured self-hosted server. We do not intercept, collect, or have access to this data.
- **Authentication Credentials:** Your `hu-` or `lb-` API keys and server URLs are stored locally on your device's secure storage. They are never sent to ClawStack Mobile Studios.
- **Telemetry and Analytics:** The App does not contain any third-party analytics trackers, crash reporters, or telemetry SDKs. We do not track your usage behavior.

## 2. Permissions

To function correctly, the App requires the following device permissions:
- **Internet Access:** Required to communicate with your self-hosted server to fetch and save your pinchmarks.
- **Camera (Optional):** May be requested strictly if you choose to scan a QR code for your API key. This data is processed locally on your device and never transmitted.

## 3. Data Retention

Because we do not collect any of your data, we do not retain any of your data. To delete your data from the App, you may simply log out, clear the App's data from your device settings, or uninstall the App. To permanently delete your pinchmarks, you must manage them directly within your self-hosted Habitat.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with third-party advertising networks or data brokers. All network requests made by the App are directed exclusively to the custom server URL you provide during the setup wizard. 

*Note: If you use the built-in "Reading Mode" feature, your self-hosted server may interact with `r.jina.ai` to convert articles to markdown, but the mobile App does not interact with this service directly.*

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy.
